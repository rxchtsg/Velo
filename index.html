How it works
Three steps.
Zero friction.

The whole pipeline runs in the background so you can focus on the conversation, not the technology.

1

You speak

Velo captures your voice in real time using Whisper, OpenAI's speech recognition model. No button pressing, no delays — just talk.

2

It translates

The transcribed text is sent to the DeepL API, which returns a high-quality translation in the target language within milliseconds.

3

They hear

The translated text is converted back to natural speech and played out loud on the other end. The whole loop takes under two seconds.

4

And vice versa

The same process runs in reverse simultaneously. Both sides of the conversation are translated as they happen — fully bidirectional.

Features
Built for real conversations,
not demos.

Every design decision was made with actual use cases in mind — not hypothetical ones.

⚡

Low latency pipeline

The STT, translation, and TTS pipeline is designed to run end-to-end in under two seconds, keeping conversation flow natural.

🎙️

Voice-first

No typing required. Velo is built around speech input and output — designed for hands-free, natural use during calls or meetings.

🔁

Bidirectional

Both speakers are translated simultaneously. It's not one-way — the conversation flows naturally in both directions at once.

🇩🇪

English and German first

Starting with German and English as the core language pair, with the architecture designed to support more languages down the line.

📞

Call-ready

Designed with phone calls in mind — doctor appointments, customer service, official calls — where you can't afford miscommunication.

🧩

Modular backend

The STT, translation, and TTS modules are fully separated, making it easy to swap out individual components as better models emerge.
